10FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this workshop online or offline?
It is an in-person workshop held in Bengaluru.
Who is this workshop for?
Medical school students & MBBS graduates who are exploring or already preparing for the AMC pathway to Australia.
Do I need to have started AMC preparation?
No. The day starts with an introduction to AMC, so beginners can follow along. Doctors already preparing will benefit from the exam prep and Q&A sessions.
Will this workshop help me clear the AMC exams?
The workshop gives you orientation and direction on the pathway and preparation approach. It is not exam training.
Is this workshop CPD certified?
No. This workshop is not CPD certified. It is an overview session on the AMC pathway, not exam training or a certificate course.
What should I bring?
A notebook, your questions, and details of your qualification if you want pathway-specific guidance.
