Why New Zealand?

New Zealand currently has a shortage of approximately 1,700 doctors — projected to reach 3,400 by 2032. IMGs make up 43% of the total medical workforce and 70–71% of all new doctor registrations in recent years.

The demand is real. The opportunity is structural. And it is long-term.

But knowing New Zealand needs doctors is only the starting point. Knowing exactly how to get there is what makes the difference.
